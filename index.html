Set up SMBDM Agent on your Mac

Use your own Hermes Agent from your phone, with notifications. Version 0.8.0.

Before you start

  • Hermes Agent is installed on this Mac.
  • Tailscale is on this Mac and your phone, signed in with your work account, and says Connected.

Install

Open the Terminal app, paste this line and press Return:

curl -fsSL https://smbdm-team.github.io/smbdm-agent-get/install | bash

Choose a username and a password (at least 12 characters) when it asks. At the end it shows your personal link: open it on your phone and add it to the Home Screen. Then turn on Push notifications in the app's Settings.

Update or repair

Paste the same line again. Your sign-in, link and chats stay the same.

Remove

curl -fsSL https://smbdm-team.github.io/smbdm-agent-get/install | bash -s -- --uninstall
